# Figure captions - Methods & Results draft, Figures 1, 2, 3, 7, 8, 9, 14, 15

Generated by `scripts/71_report_figures_2.py` (companion to `scripts/69_final_figures.py`, which produced Figures 4, 5, 6, 10-13 as F1-F8). Numbers are recomputed from the catalogues, grids and measurement files at build time except where the caption names the note they are quoted from.

**Figure 1 - location map.** Bathymetry throughout is the 30 m Orca multibeam grid inside its footprint and GEBCO_2023 outside it - the same surface and the same precedence used to un-shear the velocity grid (`scripts/41_build_unsheared_velgrid.py::sample_bathymetry`) - and every station position is from `catalogs/station_geometry.csv`. (a) Regional setting of the whole 38-station network: 22 ZX ocean-bottom seismometers (vermillion triangles) and 16 land and island stations of networks 5M/AI/AM (white squares, labelled), with the coast in black and the 1000 m isobath in grey. (b) The ZX array, filled shading and grey contours every 250 m; OBS are shaded by water depth (range 0.785-1.943 km, taken as the negative `elevation_m`) and the star marks the Orca caldera. (c) The dense cluster on the edifice - the 15 instruments within 9 km of the NLLoc projection origin -62.4413/-58.44, a 19 x 14 km footprint that supplies almost all of the catalogue - over the 30 m grid contoured every 50 m, with the 1000 m isobath picking out the caldera rim. Scale bars 20 km in (b) and 2 km in (c).

**Figure 2 - picker benchmark.** All of (a)-(c) are the benchmark metrics embedded in `notes/picker_benchmark.html` (`const DATA`, generated 2026-09-11 from `benchmark_metrics.csv`), i.e. the sweep data ARE on disk and are replotted here rather than transcribed. Evaluation set: the ten days from 2019-10-01 carrying the most analyst picks - 1,236 P and 2,042 S picks over 447 events, 20 ZX stations, 220 station-days per model - scored by greedy nearest-time matching per station and phase (0.5 s for P, 1.0 s for S). (a) P and S recall for the twelve models, all run at a common probability floor of 0.1; the best single model on P is PhaseNet diting at 80.6%, and the best on S is PickBlue PhaseNetLight at 93.2%. (b) Median signed pick-time residual with MAD whiskers for the four models with the highest P recall; a late S bias maps directly into S-P and therefore into depth, which is why OBSTransformer's +70 ms S was rejected. (c) Recall of every one-, two- and three-model pool against detection volume (log axis); the adopted pool, PhaseNet DiTing + PickBlue PhaseNetLight with both contributing P and S, is ringed. (d) is NOT from the benchmark: it is the timing residual of the delivered production picks against all 39,660 matched analyst picks of `catalogs/manual_pick_recall_year_newpool.csv` (`res_raw` = auto - manual on `hit_raw` rows), split by phase and by the trusted window (from 2019-10-01) against the earlier, less carefully picked part of the analyst set.

**Figure 3 - velocity model.** The production P model, `nlloc/model/ORCA_v4.P.mod` (576 x 451 x 64 nodes at 0.4 km, stored as SLOW_LEN so Vp = dx / value), in the NLLoc frame `TRANS SIMPLE -62.4413 -58.44 36`; grid +x therefore runs 54 deg east of north (along-strike) and grid +y -36 deg (across-strike). (a) Horizontal slice at 2.0 km below sea level over the central 64 x 44 km of the grid, Vp 2.01-5.51 km/s in view; the white line is the 1000 m isobath of `ORCA_v4.water_depth_km.npy`, the seafloor sampled on this same grid. (b, c) Two orthogonal sections through the Orca caldera (yellow line) along the grid axes. The black line is the seafloor from the same array; the hatched blue band above it is the water column, which this grid fills with rock velocity rather than water (a deliberate choice of the model build, so the slice colours there are not a measurement). The vermillion hatch is the resolved interval, the 4 km below the seafloor within which the model is resolved (draft sec. 9); below it Vp relaxes toward the 1D starting model. Triangles are the ZX OBS lying within 6 km of each section, plotted at their true instrument depths - the same values that reproduce the grid's own water depth at those nodes to better than 0.04 km.

**Figure 7 - cross-correlation.** (a, top) Distribution of the 103,056 delivered differential times of `hypodd/year_v6_3d_xc/dtcc_measurements.npz` (55,463 P at median cc 0.860, 47,593 S at 0.847) in 64,994 event pairs, 1.87% of the 5,511,862 catalogue rows; the file is cut at the production threshold cc >= 0.8, so no true-pair distribution exists below it. (a, bottom) The null test that fixed that threshold, quoted from notes/30_xcorr_dtcc.md sec. D3 and NOT recomputed - `scripts/63_xcorr_dtcc.py` has a `--self-test` but no null-test entry point. At station BRA22 over juldays 200-225, 2,989 real rows were rescored against the same rows with the second window randomly re-paired: purity rises from 0.52 at cc >= 0.6 through 0.74 at 0.7 to 0.79 at the adopted 0.8, and the median coefficient of unrelated windows is already 0.40. (b) The correction the correlation applies, dt.cc - dt.ct, binned by coefficient for P and S with MAD whiskers and the bin count above each point; over all rows the median is +3.0 ms with a MAD of 52.9 ms, a near-zero bias on a scatter consistent with a 35-40 ms per-pick error entering a difference of two picks. (c) Median nearest-neighbour 3D separation of the 8,005 QC-pass events of `catalogs/hypodd_year_v6_3d_xc_qc.csv`, binned by how many correlation links each event ended with (nccp + nccs); the separation falls monotonically with link count, from 0.175 km for the 1,805 events that ended with no correlation link to 0.040 km at 100-199 links (n = 91) and 0.025 km above 200 (n = 7), against 0.115 km for the catalogue as a whole - the dose-response that says the correlation data are doing real work where they are dense.

**Figure 8 - depth uncertainty budget.** (a) The three contributions to depth uncertainty, all as functions of depth below sea level, on one logarithmic axis. Blue and vermillion lines with shaded p10-p90 bands: the NLLoc formal marginal sigma_z of the delivered standard (12,194 events) and strict (3,864) tiers, binned on the same depth bins used by the perturbation study. Purple: the station-term envelope - the largest |dz| per event over the three perturbed term sets - quoted from notes/31_final_catalogue_v6.md sec. 2 (median 0.10 km in 0-1 km rising to 1.82 km at 9-15 km, p90 up to 4.94 km). Vermillion diamonds: the absolute-versus-relative disagreement, recomputed here from the join of `catalogs/nlloc_year_v6_standard.csv` with `catalogs/hypodd_year_v6_3d_qc.csv` on event_idx (hypoDD id - 1), 9,125 events. The three curves cross near 4 km: above it the formal sigma_z dominates, below it the station-term envelope and the estimator disagreement both exceed it, which is why depths below ~4 km beneath the seafloor are quoted as upper bounds. (b) The same perturbation study broken out by term set, median |dz| with whiskers to p90; the x1.2 set is the one the S-P spread gate still accepts, the two corrections-only sets are rejected by it and serve as an outer bound.

**Figure 9 - processing accounting.** Waterfall of the full chain, every number counted by `scripts/68_catalogue_accounting.py` from the obs blocks, the per-event `.hyp` files and the catalogue itself, and reproduced in notes/31_final_catalogue_v6.md sec. 3. (a) 98,631 associated events lose 18,848 to the airgun exclusion window (2019-01-21 to 2019-02-05), leaving 79,783 offered to NLLoc; 50 obs events produce no solution and 10 `.hyp` files carry no parsable GEOGRAPHIC line, giving the 79,723 rows of `catalogs/nlloc_year_v6.csv`, of which 220 are REJECTED - so 79,503 LOCATED solutions, 99.6% of the events offered (the last three cuts remove 280 events in total and are too small to render at this scale). (b) The three delivered tiers cut from those solutions: loose 15,184, standard 12,194 and strict 3,864 events (19.1%, 15.3% and 4.9% of LOCATED). The binding constraints are azimuthal gap and grid-face pinning, not pick count or rms. (c) The pick side of the same accounting: of 409,677 S picks, 20,140 (4.9% of all S, 2.5% of all picks) were removed by the S-before-P guard in 13,979 events (17.5%), deleting no events; the 409,090 P picks are untouched.

**Figure 14 - relative geometry.** The cross-correlation-refined relative catalogue `catalogs/hypodd_year_v6_3d_xc_qc.csv` (8,005 QC-pass events) at full resolution, every event coloured by origin time (built from the yr/mo/dy/hr/mi/sc columns) over 398 days from 2019-01-12 to 2020-02-14. (a) Map of the 7,944 events within +/-8 km of the NLLoc projection origin -62.4413/-58.44 (grey dots: the 61 outside the frame), over the 30 m Orca bathymetry contoured every 100 m, with the ZX OBS and the traces of the two sections. (b) W-E and (c) S-N sections through the same origin, each taking events within +/-8 km of the section line, with the seafloor profile sampled from the same bathymetry and the OBS at their true depths. The median nearest-neighbour 3D separation of this catalogue is 0.115 km, the scale at which the lineations and sub-clusters in these panels are resolved. A few events plot above the seafloor line in (b) and (c): quality control removes events above their OWN local seafloor, while the line drawn is the profile along the section, so an event projected in from off-line can sit above it. Depths are below sea level and inherit the absolute frame, so the panels show relative geometry, not absolute depth accuracy.

**Figure 15 - validation.** (a) Per-station distribution of the analyst's own S-P measurements (17,315 ZX event/station pairs with both a P and an S hand pick, 15 stations with n >= 20; box p25-p75, median in blue, whiskers p5-p95) against that station's S-P term from `nlloc/delays/v6_it1B.delays` (vermillion diamond, S delay minus P delay), stations ordered by the term; the axis is clipped at 3.6 s, above which a handful of mis-paired analyst measurements run to 119 s. The terms played no part in the analyst picks, so this is an out-of-sample test: only 83 of 17,315 pairs (0.5%) measure an S-P smaller than their own station's term (the count and percentage above each diamond, matching the 83 of 17,347 of notes/31 sec. 1), i.e. the terms never exceed what the analyst actually measured. (b) Automatic minus analyst S-P on the 13,114 pairs where the production pool matched both phases (`res_raw` of `catalogs/manual_pick_recall_year_newpool.csv`, S residual minus P residual), median +20 ms with a MAD of 50 ms; the inset gives the per-station median. (c) Recall of the analyst picks per station in the trusted window (from 2019-10-01, 11,308 picks), for the raw pick pool (solid, 90.9% overall) and for the picks that survive into the associated catalogue (hatched, 59.6%) - the gap is what association and the S-before-P guard remove, not what the picker missed.
