# Figure captions - final Orca / Bransfield catalogue (NLLoc v6)

Generated by `scripts/69_final_figures.py`. Numbers are recomputed from the catalogues and validation runs at build time, not copied from the notes.

**F1 - epicentre map.** Epicentres of the NLLoc catalogue over the Orca volcanic edifice. Grey dots: standard tier (gap < 180 deg, rms < 0.5 s, Nphs >= 6, depth_bsf > -0.2 km; 12,194 events, 11,834 inside the map frame). Coloured dots: strict tier (3,864 events, 3,838 in frame), shaded by hypocentral depth below sea level (0-10 km). Triangles are stations - filled vermillion for ZX ocean-bottom seismometers (true water depths 0.785-1.943 km), open for land stations. Thin grey lines are bathymetry contours every 250 m, from the 30 m Orca multibeam grid inside its footprint and GEBCO_2023 outside it (the same surface and precedence used to un-shear the velocity grid, scripts/41). The star marks the Orca caldera (the 30 m grid's summit is -615 m at -62.446/-58.397). Scale bar 5 km; the inset shows the full station network with the map frame outlined. Seismicity concentrates in and immediately around the caldera, where the array aperture is smallest and the azimuthal gap is therefore lowest - the binding QC constraint (notes/31 sec.3).

**F2 - depth sections.** Hypocentres within +/-15 km along and +/-5 km across two vertical sections through the NLLoc projection origin (-62.4413, -58.44), oriented along the tomography grid axes: SW-NE (N54E, along the Bransfield rift; left) and SE-NW (N36W, across it; right). Top row: NLLoc strict tier (3,830 of 3,864 events in the box). Bottom row: the hypoDD 3D double-difference relocation of the standard tier, QC-pass only (8,921 of 9,125), plotted on its depth_bsl_km column so both rows share the sea-level datum. The black line is the seafloor sampled along the section line from the same bathymetry as F1, and the pale band above it is the water column. All four panels share identical axes. Events plotted above the black line are not in the water: the profile is the seafloor ON the section line, while the points are projected onto it from up to 5 km off-line, where the seafloor is deeper (510 of the 9,125 hypoDD events, 5.6%, do sit up to 0.20 km above their own local seafloor, which is the half-grid-cell tolerance the QC allows). Seismicity is concentrated in the upper ~5 km beneath the edifice; the hypoDD panels are tighter laterally but move the 6-9 km population up by about 2 km (README sec.5 caveat 7), so absolute depth should be read from the NLLoc panels and relative geometry from the hypoDD panels.

**F3 - depth distributions.** Histograms of depth below the LOCAL seafloor (depth_bsf_km for NLLoc, depth_bsf_local_km for hypoDD), 0.25 km bins over 0-12 km, one panel per catalogue. Dotted / solid / dashed vertical lines are the p10 / p50 / p90 of the full column (a handful of events fall outside the 0-12 km plotting range and are excluded from the bars but not from the percentiles). NLLoc loose tier p10/p50/p90 = -0.01/1.64/4.90 km (n = 15,184); NLLoc standard tier p10/p50/p90 = 0.02/1.81/4.78 km (n = 12,194); NLLoc strict tier p10/p50/p90 = 0.75/2.29/4.30 km (n = 3,864); hypoDD 3D QC-pass p10/p50/p90 = 0.15/2.15/3.89 km (n = 9,059). Tightening the QC deepens the median (the strict tier additionally requires depth_bsf > +0.2 km, which removes the seafloor-hugging population) and narrows the distribution, as expected if the shallow excess is location noise rather than structure. Note the README caveat that the P tomography is honestly resolved only to about 4 km below the seafloor: 14.8% of the standard tier and 12.7% of the strict tier sit deeper than that.

**F4 - station terms and their observational support.** (a) The 76 LOCDELAY terms of nlloc/delays/v6_it1B.delays (VELEST invB, refined by one iteration of NLLoc per-station median residuals), applied by NLLoc as obs - delay, so a positive term means the station observes late. ZX OBS are ordered by water depth (green diamonds, right axis); land and island stations are grouped to the right of the dashed divider. The OBS S terms are systematically larger than their P terms - it is that S-P difference (median +0.37 s) that carries much of the depth axis, and it is a fitted quantity, not an independent observation (README caveat 2). (b) The analyst's own picks tested against those terms: for each ZX OBS with at least 20 manual P+S pairs (17,347 pairs in total), the 5th percentile and the median of the hand-measured S-P are plotted against the station's S-P delay, with the 1:1 line. Every station's p5 sits above its delay, by +0.07 s (the station with the largest term) to +0.52 s, and only 0.5% of the 17,347 individual pairs fall below their own station's delay. The margin is smallest exactly where the term is largest, which is where it should be tight.

**F5 - the S-P depth gate.** The most nearly model-free depth observable this array has is the S-P time at the nearest station, read straight from the picks. For each event in the 2,000-event A/B sample, scripts/52_sp_depth_check.py takes the nearest station within 4 km that carries both P and S, and compares the observed S-P (blue) with the S-P predicted by straight-ray geometry at the catalogue's own hypocentre (orange), binned by depth below sea level; the scorer uses a fixed Vp/Vs of 1.88 so the two runs differ only by their positions, and the observed S-P is corrected by the run's own station terms where it has them. Bin counts are printed above the bars. Left: the reference run with no station terms (n = 1,009) - the predicted S-P spreads x3.51 from the shallowest to the deepest bin while the observation only spreads x2.80, a ratio of 1.25, i.e. a depth axis about 25% too long. Right: the production v6 configuration (n = 1,002) - x2.94 predicted against x3.09 observed, ratio 0.95. The station terms remove the stretch; this is the gate that selected it1B over the invA-scale alternatives (ratios 1.12-1.19), which it rejects (notes/31 sec.2).

**F6 - travel-time solver check.** Difference between the two forward solvers used in this study on the SAME 3D P model: the NLLoc finite-difference (Podvin-Lecomte) time grid minus the hypoDD pseudo-bending ray tracer, for 65,803 real source-station rays of the year catalogue, as a function of epicentral distance (log-scaled hexbin, vermillion line = median per distance bin). Median over all rays +116 ms; by bin: 0-2 km +98 ms (n=13,413); 2-5 km +117 ms (n=32,806); 5-10 km +136 ms (n=14,262); 10-15 km +148 ms (n=2,203); 15-20 km +143 ms (n=288); 20-25 km +136 ms (n=153); 25-30 km +85 ms (n=120); 30-40 km +90 ms (n=990); 40-50 km +95 ms (n=335); 50-60 km +66 ms (n=285). The shaded band is the independently measured NLLoc-FD bias against a pykonal eikonal solver (+108 ms at 0-2 km rising to +161 ms at 10-25 km on 4,954 rays, notes/27 I21) - the tracer-based difference plotted here falls in the same place and grows with distance the same way, i.e. the two independent solvers agree with each other and Grid2Time does not. This is why the v5/v6 travel-time grids (nlloc/time/ORCA_v5.*) are built with pykonal FMM instead of Grid2Time; a 0.1-0.16 s station- and distance-dependent bias in P (inherited by S x1.78) is exactly the error that stretches a depth axis. Per-ray pykonal times were not retained, so only the FD-minus-tracer difference can be replotted here.

**F7 - temporal evolution.** (a) Daily count of standard-tier events over the 2019-01-01 to 2020-03-01 ZX deployment (bars, left axis) with the cumulative count (black, right axis); 12,194 events over 404 days, busiest day 2019-02-11 with 100 events. The shaded band is the 2019-01-21 to 2019-02-05 airgun survey window, which is excluded from the catalogue wholesale rather than filtered (18,848 associated events removed, an estimated ~1,800 of them genuine earthquakes) - the catalogue is NOT complete across it and no rate inside it should be read from this figure. (b) Depth below sea level against time for the strict tier (3,864 events), same time axis. Activity is episodic rather than steady, and the depth distribution of the well-constrained subset does not drift systematically over the deployment, which argues against a slowly changing clock, geometry or station-term error masquerading as depth.

**F8 - NLLoc vs hypoDD depth.** hypoDD 3D relocated depth against the NLLoc standard-tier starting depth for the 9,125 events that pass hypoDD QC, joined on event_idx (hypoDD `id` = event_idx + 1, set in scripts/22_pyocto_to_hypodd_input.py); both axes are below sea level, so the hypoDD depth_bsl_km column is used, not `dep`. Log-scaled hexbin with the 1:1 line and the binned median (vermillion, bars p25-p75). Regressing the change on the starting depth gives a slope of -0.275 with a median |dz| of 0.51 km and Spearman r = 0.787: the double-difference solution still compresses the deep end of the absolute depth axis. Median shift by starting-depth bin, median signed (hypoDD - NLLoc): 0-2 km +0.21 km (n=2,512); 2-4 km +0.12 km (n=4,031); 4-6 km -0.47 km (n=1,858); 6-9 km -1.96 km (n=586); 9-15 km -0.42 km (n=125). This is the residual the README flags as caveat 7 - it is concentrated in the 6-9 km population, the same bin that is least stable against perturbing the station terms (notes/31 sec.2), and it is the reason absolute depth should be quoted from NLLoc and only relative geometry from hypoDD.
