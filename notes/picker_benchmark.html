Which picker hears the Bransfield?

Recall against the analyst catalogue

Every picker ran at the same probability floor of 0.1, so the comparison is like-for-like. A pick counts as recovered when it lands within 0.5 s of an analyst P or 1.0 s of an analyst S, on the same station.

Recall against pick volume

Recall alone rewards a picker for firing constantly. This is the honest trade: how many picks per station-day each model emits to buy the recall it gets. Down and to the right is expensive; up and to the left is cheap. Each point on a line is one probability threshold, from 0.9 at the left to 0.1 at the right.

Timing accuracy

Recall says a pick was found; this says whether it was found in the right place. Bars show the median signed residual (picker minus analyst) with the median absolute deviation as the whisker. A systematic offset propagates straight into S–P times, and from there into depth.
