# Session 2026-05-19 — Hand-picking workflow, skill, Vp/Vs calibration

## State at start

Yesterday closed with the v2 NLLoc run (38 stations, extended grid,
depth-below-seafloor datum). Two Vp/Vs values had been tested in passing:
1.78 (the value baked into `configs/velocity_model.csv`) and 2.10 (a holdover

## What we did

### 1. Sample record-section workflow (`scripts/41_plot_event_record_sections.py`)

For 10 random no-shots events with `picks >= 15`, drew per-event record
sections of all available Z-component traces ordered by epicentral distance,
with pyocto pick overlays. Stations *without* picks are still drawn (gray) so
missed arrivals are visible. Default bandpass 3–10 Hz, ±30 s window.

The script handles day-crossings, missing files, and stations not in
station_geometry. Output: `notes/figures/associations/event_<idx>_<UTC>.png`.

### 2. Hand-picking experiments on three events

**Event 14341** (2.5 km below seafloor, on volcano summit, 17 pyocto picks).
At 3–10 Hz the seismologist picks matched pyocto to ~20 ms on the
overlapping picks. Added 11 picks pyocto missed (BRA13 P+S, BRA05 P+S,
BRA22 P, BRA23 P+S, BRA26 P+S, missed S on BRA16/BRA18/BRA24/BRA27).
Identified one pyocto error: BRA20 S at 0.79 s (only 0.3 s after its P) is
mislabeled; real S is at ~4 s. The BRA13 picks are also non-trivial:
the late arrival at 6.5 s pairs with a small earlier P at 3.65 s, giving
Vp/Vs=1.78 but implying BRA13 is recording an event ~20 km away — *not*
the 0.6 km distance pyocto's location implies. Suggests the event 14341
"association" mixes signal from two physically distinct sources.

**Event 26819** (5.6 km bsf, 16 pyocto picks). Cleaner geometry. Matched
pyocto to ~10 ms on overlaps. Added 4 missed picks. Several un-picked
stations (BRA19, BRA05, BRA15, BRA13, BRA09) showed *continuous pre-event
activity* — picking those would have mixed in arrivals from another event,
so they were correctly skipped.

**Event 14115** (8.1 km bsf, 15 pyocto picks). At 3–10 Hz my first attempt
catastrophically failed: I picked the loud S arrival as P, and a later coda
as S. Implied Vp came out at 3.4 km/s — physically impossible. Median P
error vs pyocto: **0.86 s**. Median S error: **1.63 s**.

Diagnosis: emergent P first-motions on a deeper event get lost against the
later impulsive S; the eye is drawn to the wrong arrival.

### 3. Built the seismologist-pick skill (`~/.claude/skills/seismologist-pick/SKILL.md`)

Documented the workflow as an agentic skill. Triggers on "look at picks",
"review picks", "hand-pick", "act as a seismologist", "can you do better
than pyocto", etc.

After the 14115 failure, the skill was upgraded with three concrete
safeguards:

- **Step 5a — moveout sanity check**: compute `Vp = hypo_dist / tP` for each
  candidate P. Reject any pick implying Vp outside 4–7 km/s (upper crust).
  The 14115 wrong picks all gave Vp ≈ 3.4 km/s — would have been caught
  immediately.
- **Step 5b — stretched-y pre-onset zoom**: render the pre-arrival segment
  with y-axis at ±6σ of pre-event noise. Forces the small first-motion
  break to be visible (it gets washed out when normalized against the loud
  later arrival).
- **Step 6a — anti-anchoring two-pass**: pick blind first, reveal picker
  picks second, write a justification for every disagreement. Stops the
  "pyocto rubber-stamp" failure mode.
- **Multi-band probing**: try 1–8, 2–10, 3–10, 5–15 Hz and pick the band
  with cleanest pre-event flatlines and sharpest first motions. For
  deeper Bransfield events (>5 km bsf), 5–15 Hz works much better than
  3–10. For shallow Orca summit events (<3 km bsf), 3–10 stays preferred.

Re-running 14115 with the safeguards:
- Median P error: 0.86 s → **0.01 s** (sample-perfect)
- Median S error: 1.63 s → 0.04 s on consensus picks
- 3 P picks added that pyocto had missed (BRA15, BRA18, BRA19)
- 3 pyocto S picks flagged as physically impossible (BRA15/BRA19/BRA21
  implied Vs < 2.5 km/s — they were caught at later peaks, not S onsets)

The safeguards work.

### 4. Empirical Vp/Vs calibration (the actual decision input for NLLoc)

Used pyocto picks on all 31,516 no-shots events to compute station-by-station
`tS/tP` ratios (assuming pyocto origin times). 106,841 P+S station-event
pairs in the physical range [1.3, 2.5].

- **All station-event pairs (median): Vp/Vs = 1.778**
- **Per-event medians (28,096 events, ≥3 P+S pairs each): median = 1.779,
  IQR = 1.748–1.804**
- **Depth dependence is essentially flat:**

  | Depth bin (km bsf) | Median Vp/Vs |
  |---|---|
  | 0–3 | 1.789 |
  | 3–6 | 1.781 |
  | 6–10 | 1.761 |
  | 10–15 | 1.771 |
  | 15–26 | 1.779 |

The 2.10 ratio from the original NLLoc template is not supported by the
data. **Vp/Vs = 1.78 is the right value** for the final NLLoc relocation —
matching the `configs/velocity_model.csv` value already in use.

Output: `notes/figures/vpvs_population.png` (full-catalog histogram +
depth dependence).

## New scripts

- `scripts/40_filter_nlloc_reliable.py` — emits a QC-filtered subset of any
  NLLoc catalog at one of three tiers. Default is `--tier standard`.

  | Tier | Cuts | Count (v2 1.78) |
  |---|---|---|
  | loose | gap<200°, RMS<0.7s, Nphs≥4, interior of grid | 8,993 |
  | **standard** (default) | gap<180°, RMS<0.5s, Nphs≥6, interior | **7,272** |
  | strict | gap<120°, RMS<0.3s, Nphs≥8, σ_x/σ_y < 1 km, σ_z < 2 km, inside ZX station hull, depth bsf > 0.2 km | 2,050 |

  Output: `catalogs/nlloc_<label>_reliable.csv` (overwrites by default; pass
  `--tier` to change which subset is written). Figure files named
  `..._reliable.png` / `..._reliable.mp4` are the visualizations of whichever
  tier was selected at write time.

## Decisions for the paper-track

1. **Use Vp/Vs = 1.78** for the final NLLoc catalog. Empirically confirmed
   by the population of 106k S/P pairs. No depth dependence worth modelling.
2. **v2 (1.78, 38 stations, extended grid) is the canonical NLLoc run.**
   The **standard-tier reliable subset** (7,272 events; gap<180°, RMS<0.5s,
   Nphs≥6, interior of grid) is the paper-ready set. The stricter 2,050-
   event tier is available for σ-tight applications (focal mechanisms,
   detailed structure imaging).
3. The seismologist-pick skill is the QC tool for individual flagged events
   (e.g., the BRA13/14341 anomalies suggesting mixed-source associations).
   Not a replacement for PhaseNet at population scale.
