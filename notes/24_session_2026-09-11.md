# Session 2026-09-11 — Picker benchmark: 12 models against the analyst catalogue

## Goal

Started as "the pickers aren't finding all the P and S waves" — quantify that,
find out why, then test whether anything in the SeisBench zoo does better than
the production stack on Bransfield OBS data. Ended with a 12-picker benchmark,
a recommended pick pool, and a pandas-3 landmine that blocks re-running the
pipeline at all.

### 1. Quantified the recall problem

Matched the analyst catalogue (`catalogs/manual_picks.csv`, 46,339 picks in
the ML coverage window) against the production pick sets. Greedy nearest-time
match per station-phase, P ±0.5 s / S ±1.0 s.

| picker | P recall | S recall |
|---|---|---|
| PhaseNet `instance` | 46.3% | 46.2% |
| OBSTransformer | 66.2% | 89.9% |
| production pool (PN-inst P + OBST P/S) | **70.4%** | **89.9%** |

So ~30% of analyst P picks never reach the catalogue; S is in decent shape.
Splitting the misses: for P, **17.1% have no OBST pick within 5 s** (detection
failure) and **16.5% have one 0.5–5 s away** (detected, mis-timed). For S only
6.1% are true non-detections.

Two side results:
- **OBSTransformer S picks run systematically late** (+50 ms median on the
  early set, +70 ms on the late set). Every production S pick comes from OBST,
  so the whole catalogue carries that into S–P times and therefore depth.
- **Adding PhaseNet's discarded S picks buys only +0.9 points** (90.4 → 91.3%).
  `17_pyocto_associate.py` drops PhaseNet S by design; that is not what's
  hurting us.

> **Analysis bug worth remembering.** The first pass reported ~98% recall with
> median residuals of 0.0001 s — finer than one sample at 100 Hz. Cause: under
> pandas 3, `to_datetime` returns **microsecond** dtype, so `.astype("int64")`
> gives µs, and dividing by 1e9 made every residual 1000× too small — the
> ±0.5 s tolerance behaved like ±500 s. See §5.

### 2. PickBlue — the cached OBS weights, finally run

SeisBench's `obs` weights (PhaseNet / EQTransformer / PhaseNetLight) are
PickBlue: OBS-retrained, component order **`Z12H`**, i.e. three seismometer
channels **plus the hydrophone**, with a 0.5 Hz highpass on the H channel and
per-channel std normalisation. `targets.yaml` already *downloaded* `EDH`/`HDH`
but excluded them from `picking_channels`.

Added `--picking-channels` to `scripts/03_run_phasenet.py` to let them through.
Verified the 4th channel does real work — 4C vs 3C ablation on 2019-192:
P 50.7% vs 46.3%, S 67.0% vs 56.0%.

### 4. Pools — what to actually run

Exhaustive search over every 1-, 2- and 3-model pool. Models fail on
*different* arrivals, so pools beat their members by more than either
contributes alone.

| pool | P | S | picks/sta-day |
|---|---|---|---|
| current production (PN-inst P + OBST P/S) | 77.8% | 92.4% | 1,924 |
| `diting` alone | 80.6% | 84.6% | **947** |
| **`diting` + PickBlue-PhaseNetLight** ← recommended | **89.2%** | **96.0%** | 3,016 |
| OBST + `diting` + PB-PNL | 91.1% | 97.6% | 4,730 |

**Recommendation: `diting` + PickBlue-PhaseNetLight, both feeding P and S.**
+11.4 points of P and +3.6 of S over production for 1.6× the pick volume. It
also drops OBSTransformer's +70 ms S bias in favour of PhaseNetLight's +21 ms.
The 3-model pool adds 1.9 points of P for another 1,700 picks/station-day.

### 5. pandas-3 landmine — blocks re-running anything

The pod image moved to **pandas 3.0.3 on 2026-06-29**, where `to_datetime`
returns `datetime64[us]` instead of `[ns]`. Every `.astype("int64")` on a
datetime now yields **microseconds**:

- `scripts/17_pyocto_associate.py:345` — `picks_in["time"].astype("int64") / 1e9`
  builds pyocto's epoch seconds. Now 1000× too small and **silent**: a year of
  picks collapses into ~9 hours of apparent time.
- `scripts/05_validate_picks.py:153` — mixes `Timestamp.value` (still ns) with
  `.astype("int64")` (now µs). Inflates recall wildly.
- Same pattern: `16_stalta_on_dd.py:209`, `45_compute_station_corrections.py:43`,
  `manual_picks_to_pyocto.py:48,64`, `src/bransfield_eq/manual_picks.py:298`.

The May catalogues predate the upgrade and are fine. `requirements.txt` only
says `pandas>=2.0`. **Fix these and pin pandas before re-running any stage.**

## Scope and caveats

- **10 days, not the year.** ~2.4% of deployment days, 29% of late-period ZX
  analyst picks.
- **Sampling checked, and it holds.** OBST and PN-`instance` have full-year
  picks, so the 10 days can be compared against all 123 late-period days
  (11,308 picks): OBST 73.4% vs 74.3% P, 92.4% vs 92.0% S; PN-inst 51.5% vs
  50.7% P, 52.2% vs 51.1% S. All within 1.1 points.
- **Differences under ~2 points are noise** (447 events is the effective
  independent unit). OBST and PickBlue-PhaseNetLight are **tied** on P.
- The 10 days cluster in two windows (Oct 2019, Jan 2020) — two noise regimes,
  not the full seasonal range. Station count per day varies 8–19.
- **Not a precision measurement.** The analyst catalogue holds only tectonic
  events they chose to pick, so unmatched picks are not necessarily false.
  Chance matching is ~3% at the busiest pick rate, so the large gaps are real.
- The early/late gap is a property of the **reference**, not the pickers:
  OBSTransformer scores 54.3% P on the early set vs 73.4% on the late set.

## Open

- **Fix the pandas-3 unit bugs and pin pandas.** Precondition for everything.
- **Validate the pool through association before trusting it.** Higher pick
  recall ≠ better catalogue. Run pyocto on one test month with the new pool and
  compare event count, picks/event, post-fit RMS and NLLoc σ against the
  current catalogue.
- **Full-year re-pick with the chosen pool** (~16,000 station-days/model;
  ~6 h each on this CPU-only pod, well under an hour on a GPU pod — worth
  restarting the pod with a GPU profile first).
- **Confirmation run on all 123 late days** for the top 2–3 models
  (~2,700 station-days each, ~2.7 h/model on CPU) to put the decision on
  11,308 picks instead of 3,278.
- **Re-do the fine-tune from a better base, on the late period only.** The
  existing Bransfield fine-tune gained 5.2 points of P over its base
  (PhaseNet `instance`) but **lost 12.9 points of S** — plausibly because it
  trained on the whole manual catalogue including the unreliable early picks.
  Scripts `06`–`09` already do this; the base should be `diting`, not
  `instance`, and the training window should be the late period.
- **Rebuild downstream** once the pick pool changes — pyocto, NLLoc absolutes,
  refined-pick relocations, GrowClust, hypoDD.
- Still no session notes for the 2026-06-05 and 2026-08-28→09-01 sessions
