# Session 2026-05-21 — Pick-refinement at scale + NLLoc catalog join bug

## Goal

Run the 50-event ablation queued at the top of session 22's "Open" list,
then scale the refinement workflow to the full reliable pool. Hit a
load-bearing bug along the way and had to fix the v2 NLLoc catalog
before any comparison was meaningful.

## What happened

### 1. 50-event ablation — first attempt produced nonsense

Sampled 50 events from `nlloc_picker_only_no_shots_v2_reliable.csv`,
refined picks gold-tier-relaxed (consensus PN/OBST within 0.5 s,
single-picker fallback, S–P > 0.3 s, Vp/Vs ∈ [1.4, 2.4]), re-ran NLLoc.
Result was visibly broken: median RMS = 32.6 s, median horizontal drift
8.8 km (p95 188 km), σ collapsing to ~0 km.

Tracked it to one event (`event_idx=24599`): pyocto picks at
`2019-07-17 07:26:xx`, reliable catalog OT for the same `event_idx` at
`08:44:52` — about 78 min off. The refinement queried picker CSVs at
the *wrong* origin time, mixed in picks from a neighbouring physical
event, and NLLoc converged on a degenerate origin in between.

### 2. The actual bug — `scripts/31_nlloc_hyp_to_catalog.py` join

Script 31 joined NLLoc hyp files to pyocto `event_idx` by sorting both
sides by `origin_time` and matching positionally. NLLoc occasionally
relocates an event with a large OT shift; when that happens the sort
order desynchronizes and downstream rows slip. Quantified across the
full v2 catalog: median NLLoc-vs-pyocto OT delta was +60 s, 50% of
events had |Δt| > 60 s, max +8.7 h. Roughly half the
`event_idx → hypocenter` mapping was wrong.

The reliable subset inherited this — many "reliable" rows passed the
QC thresholds because they were carrying somebody else's location.

### 3. Fix in script 31

`_collect_hyps_in_obs_order()` now detects `shard_XX/` subdirs and
reconstructs global obs_order as `local_idx * N_SHARDS + shard_id`,
then merges to `event_order.csv` on obs_order rather than sort-by-OT.

After the fix, OT delta is centered ±0 s with 99 % of events within 5 s
(361 events still drift > 5 s, which is genuine NLLoc OT shift). Two
hand-checks confirmed `event_idx=24599` now points at the right
hypocenter.

Regenerated:
- `catalogs/nlloc_picker_only_no_shots.csv` (v1, 30,462 events)
- `catalogs/nlloc_picker_only_no_shots_v2.csv` (v2, 31,515 events)
- `catalogs/nlloc_picker_only_no_shots_reliable.csv` (v1 standard, 4,319)
- `catalogs/nlloc_picker_only_no_shots_v2_reliable.csv` (v2 standard, 7,272)
- `catalogs/hybrid_picker_only_no_shots.csv` (33,988 rows; script 32)
- `catalogs/station_corrections.csv` (script 45 against corrected pool)

Counts at the QC tiers happened to be identical to the pre-fix counts
(the broken rows still passed the standard thresholds in similar
proportion), but the *identities* of the 7,272 v2-reliable rows are now
the correct events.

Also regenerated figures:

  v2_reliable (added `--tt-prefix` to script 33 so v2 grid bounds are right)

### 5. Decided to scale up

Quick triage on why 24,210 of 31,515 v2 events fail standard tier:
- 24,210 fail on **gap ≥ 180°** (76.8% of catalog)
- 2,451 fail on RMS ≥ 0.5 (7.8%)
- 3,901 are on the grid boundary (12.4%)
- 0 fail on n_phases < 6

Gap is the bottleneck — those events are physically outside the OBS
array footprint, where refinement can't help (no stations to add).
User picked "loose tier or above" as the scale-up scope (8,993 events).

### 6. Full 8,993-event refinement run

Wrote `scripts/48_refine_full_catalog.py` with an LRU cache on
per-(picker, station, julian-day) CSV loads (otherwise each event re-reads
~76 files; with caching, only ~31 k unique day-files are read across the
whole catalog). Cache hit rate ended at 95 % (662,644 hits / 31,084
misses).

Runtime:
- Refinement: ~22 min single process at ~7 ev/s steady state
- NLLoc (16 shards): ~5 min

### 7. Why the standard-tier count dropped

The drop is entirely the RMS cut. σ tightens for ~80 % of events, but
median RMS rises from 0.20 s to 0.46 s — right at the 0.5 s threshold
— so events that gained picks but couldn't fit them tighter to the 1D
model fall out.

The 0.20 s baseline RMS was artificially low because pyocto specifically
*associates picks that fit a 1D moveout*. Refinement reintroduces real
arrivals at distant 5M stations and at lower SNR — these are physically
correct picks but ones the 1D model can't reproduce within 0.1–0.2 s.
So the RMS rise reflects **model insufficiency on a broader pick set**,
not pick-quality degradation.

The right read: σ-based QC is the defensible reliable-tier criterion
for refined catalogs. σ comes from the curvature of the likelihood at
the optimum and reflects geometric constraint (azimuthal coverage,
S–P spread), not the absolute level of model misfit.

## Conceptual lessons

### The reliable-tier bottleneck is geometry, not picks

24 k of 31 k v2 events fail standard tier on `gap ≥ 180°`. These are
events outside the ZX OBS hull where no amount of pick refinement
helps — you cannot put a station where there isn't one. The pick-
refinement story is about **tightening σ on already-located events**,
not promoting marginal ones into the reliable tier.

### Don't sort to join in NLLoc pipelines

NLLoc relocates origin times. A join that sorts by OT is implicitly
trusting that NLLoc never moves OT more than the spacing between
adjacent input events. For a catalog with sub-second-resolution OTs
and ~31 k events, that spacing is ~10 s. Any NLLoc OT shift > 10 s
breaks the join silently. Use the obs_order sidecar instead.

### Pyocto picks are self-selecting on RMS

Pyocto's associator picks the subset of arrivals that fit a 1D moveout.
Post-NLLoc RMS on pyocto picks therefore underestimates the true model
misfit — any pick that didn't fit was filtered out at association time.
Refinement reintroduces those picks. Compare σ, not RMS, to judge
location quality across the two pick sets.

### Cache picker-CSV reads when scaling

The naïve per-event load_picker_picks re-reads ~76 per-day files per
event. For 8,993 events that's ~680 k file opens. An LRU cache keyed
on (picker_dir, network, station, year, doy) reduces this to ~31 k
unique reads — about 22× fewer disk hits, and turns the refinement
into a ~22-minute job instead of >8 hours.

## Open

- **σ-based reliable-tier definition.** The standard tier as currently
  defined (RMS<0.5) penalises refined catalogs unfairly. Pick a σ-based
  criterion (probably σ_x, σ_y < 1 km, σ_z < 2 km, gap < 180°, n≥6,
  not on boundary) and use it on `nlloc_refined_loose.csv`. Expect a
  larger and physically-meaningful reliable subset.
- **Downstream rebuilds with refined locations.** Once the refined
  catalog is the canonical NLLoc result, GrowClust / hypoDD inputs and
  the hybrid catalog should be rebuilt against it. Hypocenter shifts
  are typically ~1 km horizontally, ~0.2 km vertically — enough to
  matter for cluster structure but not catastrophic.
- **Refine the remaining ~22 k v2 events.** Most are gap≥180 events
  outside the OBS hull, but the σ improvement should still hold. The
  question is whether they meaningfully add anything to the catalog
  or whether they remain "outside-the-array" events the refinement
  can't rescue.
- **The 361 events with |OT delta| > 5 s after the fix** are genuine
  NLLoc OT shifts (likely far-distance or under-constrained events).
  Worth flagging in QC as a small risk pool.
